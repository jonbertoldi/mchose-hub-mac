# Third party material

## HRTF data (`app/hrir48.bin`)
Derived from the MIT KEMAR "compact" HRTF measurements (elevation 0, azimuths 30, 90, 135 degrees; mirrored for the left side; resampled from 44.1 kHz to 48 kHz).

Gardner, W. G. and Martin, K. D. "HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone", MIT Media Lab, 1994.
Source: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html
The data may be used freely provided the source is cited.

## EQ presets (`app/Presets.json`)
The 10-band preset gain values (game and music) mirror the numbers the official MCHOSE M HUB publishes for this headset.
They are plain numeric gains. Preset names are translated to English.
If you are the rights holder and want this removed, please open an issue.
